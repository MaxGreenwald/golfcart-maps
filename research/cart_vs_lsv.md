# Plain golf cart vs LSV: rule split across all 37 regions (branch cart-vs-lsv)

**Finding:** master uses only the state `lsv` profile (`setRule(): RULE={...st.lsv,...}`) and labels every green road "OK for carts & LSVs".
A plain golf cart (no VIN, not FMVSS 500) has a stricter rule in most states. The CA rule is CVC 21716: only on roads posted ≤25, unless a CVC 21115 /
Sts. & Hwys. §1950 golf cart plan designates other routes. So in CA, AND in GA, NC, FL, HI and ME, the map tells plain-golf-cart drivers that roads they may not use are OK.
(A mode switch existed before and was removed on 2026-10-14, in 2b757ad "one rule set ≤35".)

**Fix (root):** `public/roadrule.js` is the only rule module. It provides `ruleFor(rules, region, 'golf_cart'|'lsv')` and `cls(seg, rule)`. The thresholds and cites live in
`data/rules.json` (every state now has both a `golf_cart` and an `lsv` profile) and in `data/regions.json` `rule` (flat keys apply to both vehicles; `rule.golf_cart` / `rule.lsv` apply to one).
app.js, carplay.js, `scripts/cart_impact.js` and the tests all call it. The LSV classification of every live segment is identical to master (this is tested).

Reading the table:
- **Speed cap (596 mi in 6 regions) is a real mislabel.** These roads are posted 30–35 and shown "OK for carts & LSVs", but plain golf carts can't use them: SD 285, Peachtree City 142 (cap open), OC coast 97, OBX 39 (cap open), Coachella 26, St. Simons 8.
- **Florida (7,530 mi) is a data gap, not proven illegal.** F.S. 316.212 allows golf carts only on roads the county or city has designated and signed. We have designation data (OSM `golf_cart=designated`) for almost none of them. In golf cart mode, FL is mostly red until we source designation lists.
- **HI/ME (653 mi):** plain golf carts aren't street-legal. The labels there already say "street-legal (electric) LSVs", so the copy is right. Golf cart mode shows cart paths only.
- **SC, TX, AZ, OH, AL, Avalon, BHI: 0.** The golf cart cap equals the LSV cap (or the region cap already applies to both).

## Open legal questions
1. FL: designation lists per city/county (Villages/Sumter, Naples, Siesta, 30A/Walton, Destin, PCB, St. Augustine, Amelia, Key West, Sun City Center, Lakewood Ranch, Anna Maria). Also: is ≤30 (FLHSMV/FDOT guidance) or the designation itself the cap? The app uses the designation, ≤35.
2. GA: the PTV cap. The app uses 25. Peachtree City allows carts on city streets except Hwy 74/54/Peachtree Pkwy, possibly up to 35 (142 mi at stake). Glynn County (St. Simons/Jekyll) and Tybee ordinances are also open.
3. NC: rules are town by town. The app uses 25 for OBX (Duck ≤25, Nags Head ≤35, Kill Devil Hills LSVs only; the KDH ban isn't modeled). BHI/Southport/Oak Island stay at 35.
4. CA golf cart plans: Palm Desert and La Quinta (Coachella) designate some routes above 25, and Laguna Woods is in OC coast. These need route data. Avalon autoettes are a separate class.
5. AZ: are registered and insured golf carts allowed on streets ≤35 statewide, or only by local practice? (Sun City.)
6. TX: plate (551.402), 2 mi radius from where parked, daytime, and city authorization (551.404). SC: 4 mi radius and daylight. These are shown as notes only, not modeled.
7. Product decision for Max: the default vehicle. The branch defaults to LSV, which leaves today's map unchanged. Defaulting to golf cart is safer for CA, GA and NC, but makes FL mostly red.

## UI (per Max, Oct 18: "too much complex ui… a setting… default lsv")
- The chip row is unchanged from master. Golf cart mode is an on/off switch in the existing ⚙️ Settings panel in the bottom sheet, above Chargers. The subtitle shows the active rule (e.g. "On: only roads posted ≤25 mph").
- Default is LSV. The choice is saved in localStorage `gcm-veh`, and `?veh=golf_cart` sets it from a URL.
- In LSV mode, popups still flag the mislabel: "OK for LSVs (not for golf carts: only roads ≤25 mph)".
- CarPlay: `GCMCarPlay.status()` and `plan()` use the same saved setting, re-read on every call. `setMode('golf_cart'|'lsv')` changes it. The native `mode` argument (hard-coded 'lsv' in CarPlaySceneDelegate.swift) is ignored on purpose.
