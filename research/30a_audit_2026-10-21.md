# 30A / South Walton map audit, Oct 21, 2026

Trigger: South Walton Golf Cart Rentals (Oct 21): "There are areas on there that do not allow carts that you have on there as good areas." They haven't named roads yet.

## Sources
- Walton County Sheriff LSV page: https://waltonso.org/lsv/ ("GOLF CARTS ARE NOT LEGALLY PERMITTED ON ANY PUBLIC ROADWAY AT ANY TIME"; LSVs only on roads <=35 mph; not on US 98 or its sidewalk; cross 98 only at 4-way intersections; no LSVs on sidewalks/bike paths; "roads prohibited to LSVs are highlighted in red").
- Sheriff map image (Oct 2022): https://waltonso.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/lsv_map_102022.png
- Walton County Ordinance 2025-03 (LSV sticker; no road list): https://www.mywaltonfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/43678/Ordinance-2025-03-LSV
- Walton County FAQ: golf carts not allowed on county-maintained roads; LSVs on <=35 mph: https://www.mywaltonfl.gov/faq.aspx?TID=32

## Method
The map image was georeferenced to our OSM data (US 98 and US 331 used as control lines; fit error ~1-2 px at 1/4 scale). Every segment the app shows as OK for LSVs was sampled every ~50 ft against the map's red lines. Overlays were checked by eye (working files in /workspace/gcm30a/).

## Checked and correct
- US 98 / Emerald Coast Pkwy and US 331: already blocked.
- County Road 30A itself: **not** red on the Sheriff map (it's posted 25-35, LSV-legal). It stays green. It is still blocked for plain golf carts, like every public road. This goes against the assumption that "30A itself is restricted": the Sheriff map doesn't support that.
- CR 393 (N and S), Mack Bayou Rd, CR 395 south of the fire station, Pine Log Rd: not red, so no change. Their speeds are still assumed (OSM untagged); see accuracy15.md.
- Golf-cart mode: Florida golf-cart rule is designated-only, and Walton designates nothing, so all public roads are already red for golf carts. What stays green in golf-cart mode is the Sandestin resort cart paths (private) and 16.4 mi of unnamed service roads that OSM tags golf_cart=yes/designated (resort or private roads). See "Uncertain".
- Cart paths in LSV mode are all inside Sandestin (private). No public bike path (e.g. Timpoochee Trail) is marked OK.

## Code change
scripts/build_region.py: a ban can be "per_segment": true, so only the stretch of a way inside the ban's bbox or lat range is blocked (needed for CR 83, where one OSM way runs from 30A to US 98). Blocked segments get code 'v' so the same-road island healer can't reopen them. No other region uses it, and their builds are unchanged.

## Uncertain
- The Sheriff map is from Oct 2022 and is an image, not a list. Street names come from the overlay, so a few short or unnamed alleys in Alys/Rosemary/Seaside may still be green.
- Why Seaside, Alys and Rosemary are red (probably private or town rules) isn't stated. We follow the map.
- The CR 83 and CR 395 ban end points are read off the image (about ±100 m).
- The 16.4 mi of OSM golf_cart-tagged unnamed service roads in golf-cart mode: probably private resort roads, not checked.
- The "Avoid" popup on these streets says "40+ mph or major road" (generic app text), though the source link shows the Sheriff map. Copy change is pending.
