# Town pages: flags (seo-town-pages, 2026-10-21)
Rules we could not source were left OFF the pages. Open items:

1. **Peachtree City (enforcement mismatch):** the page says the ordinance bars carts from Peachtree Pkwy (matches the enforcement record), but the live map still shows 0.34 mi of Peachtree Pkwy (posted 25-30) as OK for golf carts. Fix the map before or with this deploy. Which other PTC city streets carts may use is not digitized, so the page doesn't list them. The "~100 miles of paths" figure was dropped (unsourced).
2. **30A:** pages state the Walton County Sheriff's red-road bans that went live tonight (fix-30a-audit). The sheriff map is dated 10/2022.
3. **Miramar Beach / Sandestin:** these are in the 30A region (Walton County rules), right next to the Destin region, which has a known mismatch. The pages make no Destin/Okaloosa claims.
4. **Hilton Head / Bluffton:** no town-level golf cart ordinance was found. The pages use the SC state rules (SCDPS). Since May 22, 2025, S.C. 56-2-90 lets towns set hours and locations, so local rules may differ. Gated plantations (Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes) have private rules, so no page was made for them.
5. **Myrtle Beach:** the city names Kings Hwy and US 501. US 17 Bypass, SC 31/22/707/544 are listed from our ban data as "primary roads". North Myrtle Beach and Surfside Beach town ordinances were not read; their pages use the SC and city rules only.
6. **San Diego coast:** state CVC rules only. Carlsbad's ordinance-only flips are held (no specific Carlsbad roads are claimed). Del Mar's Camino del Mar 40->30 change on Nov 19, 2026 is not mentioned. Coronado is held for city email but has a page with state rules only; pull it if Coronado should stay off.
7. **The Villages / Lady Lake / Wildwood:** Sumter County's designated-road lists aren't online. The banned list (US 27/441, US 301, SR 44) comes from our data (2018 Villages-News compilation plus the District rules). The "age 14" FAQ comes from F.S. 316.212.
8. **Naples / Vanderbilt Beach / Marco Island:** the City of Naples golf cart street designations aren't digitized. The pages only say "designated and signed roads".
9. **Port Aransas / Mustang Island:** the LSV row cites Tex. Transp. Code 551.304 (<=45) while the map plans on <=35. Whether the Mustang Island (county) area outside city limits needs the city plate is unconfirmed.
10. **Sun City West / Youngtown:** the source is the Sun City HOA's MCSO guidance (written for Sun City). Sun City West (PORA) and the Town of Youngtown rules were not checked.
11. **Gulf Shores / Orange Beach:** the Orange Beach list was OCR'd and Ord. 2025-1521 wasn't fully read. Pages say only "25 mph cap" and "state highways".
12. Map centers are approximate town centers (checked to fall inside the region bbox). They were not checked on the ground.
13. No rental shops are listed on any page.
