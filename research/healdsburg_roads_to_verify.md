# Roads to verify: Healdsburg, CA (added 2026-11-22)

Posted signs win. A road listed here is not a claim that the map is wrong.

Most through streets inside the city take their speed from Healdsburg Municipal Code 10.50.050 (https://ecode360.com/47365641). Every street that section lists is 35 mph or less, so LSVs may use all of them. Plain golf carts may use the ones at 25 mph or less (CVC 21716). Untagged residential, unclassified and tertiary streets the code does not list are assumed 25 mph. Residential streets at 25 mph are on Exhibit A of 10.50.020, which is on file with the City Clerk and not online.

## Assumed 25 mph on a named collector

| Road | Why it needs a look |
|---|---|
| East Street, Johnson Street, Piper Street | Tertiary, not in 10.50.050, no posted speed in OpenStreetMap. Assumed 25 mph. A 30 mph sign would close them to plain golf carts. |
| West Grant Street west of Grove Street | Same: tertiary, assumed 25 mph. |

## Shown stricter than the code

| Road | What the map shows |
|---|---|
| Healdsburg Avenue Memorial Bridge | 30 mph with its east approach. The bridge itself is 15 mph (10.50.060). Plain golf carts see it red. |
| Healdsburg Avenue at Old Redwood Highway (southern city limit) | About 0.05 mi picks up the federal US 101 speed (70 mph) and stays red. The code posts this end of Healdsburg Avenue at 35 mph. |
| Westside Road between Mill Street and Kinley Drive | 45 mph from Mapillary signs. Westside Road is not in 10.50.050, so it stays red. |

## Not drawn

The map is clipped to the OpenStreetMap city line. Kinley Drive (35 mph in 10.50.050) and the Magnolia Drive area west of US 101 fall outside that line. The county wine roads (West Dry Creek Road, Westside Road south of town, Los Amigos Road, Lytton Station Road and others) are outside too, because unposted rural county roads would otherwise be assumed 25 mph.
