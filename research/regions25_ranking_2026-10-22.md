# Next 25 regions: ranking (2026-10-22)

Max (Oct 22): "let's also add another 25 top neighborhoods/cities". South Bay LA (`southbay`) is being built separately and isn't counted here.

**How these were ranked:** golf cart/LSV demand. That means rental-shop density and visitor volume, whether a local ordinance actually allows carts or LSVs on streets, beach, resort and retirement character, and how widely carts show up in local search and rental listings. Every pick was checked against the 38 live region bboxes so none overlaps an existing region.

**Already covered (not candidates):** Coronado (sd), Orange Beach (gulfshores), Marco Island (naples), Jekyll (goldenisles), Miramar Beach/Sandestin (destin/30a), Oak Island (bhi), Sun City West (suncity), Palm Desert (coachella) and Fernandina (amelia).

## Alternates and skips
- **Alternates:** Wrightsville Beach NC (no town LSV ordinance found), Venice/Englewood FL (no city cart designation found), Lake of the Ozarks MO, Lake Havasu AZ (legality unverified, dropped once before), Cocoa Beach FL, Sun Lakes AZ.
- **Skipped:**
  - Fenwick Island DE: LSVs are banned town-wide. It's modeled as a ban inside `delbeaches`, not as its own region.
  - Mackinac Island: no motor vehicles.
  - Kiawah, Seabrook, Fripp, Daufuskie, Sea Island and Sun City Hilton Head: private or gated roads.
  - Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket: Massachusetts caps LSVs at 30 mph, and there's little rental evidence.
  - Tellico Village, Lake Oconee/Reynolds: mostly private community roads.

## Notes
- **New states:** NJ, DE and VA get rule profiles in rules.json. NJ LSVs are limited to roads posted 25 or less (a region rule `max_posted 25`). NJ and DE plain golf carts aren't street-legal. VA golf carts need a designated street posted 25 or less.
- **Golf cart vs LSV:** where a town allows only LSVs (Topsail Beach, NJ, DE), the region's golf_cart profile is not_street_legal.
