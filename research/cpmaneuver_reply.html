How maneuvers are populated
  • Our router plans a route on the legal road network. Each step of the route becomes one CPManeuver.
  • instructionVariants: long to short, e.g. "Turn right onto Carlsbad Boulevard", "Right onto Carlsbad Boulevard", "Carlsbad Boulevard". Dashboard and notification variants are set as well.
  • symbolImage: a turn arrow chosen from the maneuver type (left, right, slight left/right, U-turn, straight, arrive).
  • initialTravelEstimates: the distance and time to reach the maneuver. While driving, CPNavigationSession.updateEstimates(_:for:) updates it from the device location.
  • upcomingManeuvers: always the current and the next maneuver, so the driver sees what comes after the turn.
  • Trip estimates: CPMapTemplate.update(_:for:with:) keeps arrival time, minutes and miles remaining current.
  • On iOS 17.4 and later we also set maneuverType, junctionType, junctionExitAngle (the real turn angle), trafficSide, roadFollowingManeuverVariants, maneuverState and currentRoadNameVariants.
The map layer is a CPMapTemplate with our own base view that shows the route and highlights roads allowed for the driver's vehicle in green and roads to avoid in red.
