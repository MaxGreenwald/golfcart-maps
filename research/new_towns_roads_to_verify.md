# Roads to verify: towns added 2026-11-13

Posted signs win. A road listed here is not a claim that the map is wrong. Untagged residential, living street, service, unclassified, and tertiary roads are assumed 25 mph. Untagged primary and secondary roads stay red (speed unknown). A DOT or FHWA speed was applied where the line matched.

These stretches need a person to read the sign before anyone treats the color as settled.
