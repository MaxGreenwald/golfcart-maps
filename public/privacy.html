Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Golf Cart Maps is operated by Max Greenwald (Matchimus LLC).

Golf Cart Maps (golfcartmap.com and our iPhone app) is a map of roads posted 35 mph or under. We collect as little as possible. There are no accounts and no ads. We do not sell the information we store.

Your location

If you allow it, your device's location is used on your device only, to show where you are, pick your nearest map area, and start routes. Your location is not sent to our servers. Your searches and routes are worked out on your device too.

Third-party services

To draw the map and answer searches, your device connects directly to these services, which see your IP address and the request (for example the map area you're viewing or the place you typed):

  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) for map images, and Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) as a backup place search.
  • Photon by Komoot (photon.komoot.io) for place search.
  • unpkg (map code library) and Google Fonts (fonts).
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, processes standard request logs, and stores our anonymous usage statistics (see below).

Road and EV charger data (from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. DOE Alternative Fuels Data Center) is packaged and served from our own site; the app doesn't send your location to the DOE.

Charger layer purchases

Payments for the charger layer are handled by Stripe. We never see your card. Stripe keeps the email you enter at checkout, and we use it only to confirm your subscription, including when you ask to restore an unlock by email. We don't keep our own database of customers. Your device stores a small unlock token so the layer stays unlocked.

Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you do, we get your email address and what you wrote, and use it only to fix the map and reply to you.

Usage statistics

To learn which towns and features help people, the website, the iPhone app and CarPlay send us a few anonymous usage events: when a visit starts, which town map you view, when you search for a route (whether one was found, and its length rounded to half a mile), when you start or finish a route, and when you open a road report (only the issue type you picked, never your notes).

  • What each event includes: the event name, the town map you're viewing (for example "Coronado"), whether you're using the website, the iPhone app or CarPlay, and a random ID. If you arrived from an ad or link, we also record the campaign tags in the link (UTM tags) and the name of the website that sent you (for example "google.com"), not the full address.
  • The random ID: your device keeps a random install ID and a visit ID that resets after 30 minutes without use, so we can count weekly users without knowing who they are. They aren't tied to your name, email, Apple ID, advertising ID or payments. Clearing site data or deleting the app resets them.
  • What these events never include: your name, email, phone number, precise location or GPS coordinates, the places you search for, your IP address (we don't store it), cookies, or anything typed into a report. These events are separate from HubSpot, described below.
  • How these events are used: only to improve Golf Cart Maps and to report totals, for example "120 people viewed the Coronado map this week." We do not use these events for advertising. Stats are stored with Cloudflare, which processes them for us. Raw events are deleted after about 13 months.
  • Opting out: if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we send no usage events.

Stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device (your last map area, your Golf cart mode setting, whether you've seen the risky-road notice, an unlock token if you've purchased, the random usage IDs described above, and your HubSpot allow or don't-allow choice) and caches map data for offline use. Clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes them.

HubSpot

On the homepage https://golfcartmap.com/ only, HubSpot's script is not loaded until you click Allow HubSpot. It is not loaded on the map, town pages, the iPhone app, or the partner admin. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, or this device is opted out of usage statistics, HubSpot stays off even if you had allowed it before.

Allowing it loads HubSpot's script for portal 52245531 (https://js.hs-scripts.com/52245531.js). That file then requests HubSpot analytics, HubSpot's cookie banner script, and HubSpot's collected-forms script. Analytics sets HubSpot cookies (their documentation names __hstc, hubspotutk, __hssc, and __hssrc) and records page views. If a chatflow is turned on in the HubSpot account, the same script can also load a chat widget. This website does not turn a chatflow on.

In the HubSpot account, Settings, Marketing, Forms, Non-HubSpot Forms, the switch "Collect data from website forms" is off. With that switch off, the collected-forms script does not send form submissions. This website does not turn that switch on.

Don't allow, or Turn it off, saves that choice on this device only. Turn it off asks HubSpot's tracking code to stop, revokes its consent cookies, and expires the analytics and chat cookies named in HubSpot's documentation (__hstc, hubspotutk, __hssc, __hssrc, messagesUtk). Later visits to the homepage do not load the script. You can change the choice here. That same homepage script is what shows the Golf Cart Map Assistant chat when the account's website channel is on. This site does not add a second chat embed, and it does not start a chat for you.

Children

Golf Cart Maps isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
